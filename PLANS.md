1. We're creating web app to learn English words.
2. Target audience is school kid, not native English speaker.
3. Words are spread on groups (e.g. family, animals, jobs etc). Create 20 popular groups.
4. For each group create 20 popular words.
5. When a kid opens the app and chooses learning she should choose a group. When a group is chosen learning starts.
6. Learning is a random set of cards with 2 sides - Russian wording, English wording + spelling. Each side has a small image corresponding to the word, the same image for both sides.
7. A kid reads one side of a card, try to guess translation, then click on card to see its another side and check herself. Make card transition animated.
9. The order English-Russian or Russian-English should be chosen by a kid when she opens a group for learning.
